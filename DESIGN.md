## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for adult educators, course coordinators, and school administrators navigating modern instructional technology. The aesthetic bridges high-tech luminescence with deep pedagogical clarity. It departs from sterile academic LMS interfaces by adopting a sleek, futuristic dark-canvas design inspired by cutting-edge digital showcase products—infusing it with luminous visual hierarchy, generous tactile affordances, and calm, distraction-free structure.

The target audience consists of mature adult education instructors, vocational mentors, and administrative staff who require:
- Maximum legibility with high-contrast type and generous letter-spacing to ease extended screen reading.
- Intuitive, confidence-inspiring spatial cues with distinct, luminous focus states and large hit areas.
- Purposeful educational visual signifiers: status pills, competency badges, mastery rings, and modular lesson containers.

The design movement combines **Futuristic Dark Minimalism** and **Refined Glassmorphism**. Deep void surfaces (`#06070B` and `#0B0D14`) are punctuated by subtle atmospheric radial glows, soft border delineations (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), and luminous semantic status accents in electric violet, cyber cyan, and pedagogical emerald.

## Colors

The color architecture is built for extended night-time or low-strain working sessions typical of educators grading, preparing curricula, or managing remote cohorts.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#633BF3`):** The primary brand driver, an electric indigo-violet evolved from `#4F1AD6`. It directs primary interactions, main CTAs, active cohort tabs, and core action pathways.
- **Secondary (`#00E5FF`):** Cyber Cyan. Used for analytical metrics, live class indicators, real-time sync states, and digital pedagogical markers.
- **Tertiary (`#00E599`):** Pedagogical Emerald. Signifies completion, mastery achieved, rubric validation, and submitted assignments.
- **Neutral (`#07080D`):** Deep obsidian abyss base that grounds all cards and modals without causing stark black/white OLED harshness.

### Pedagogical Accents
- **Amber (`#FFB300`):** Pending instructor review, draft syllabus, submission deadline warning.
- **Coral (`#FF5252`):** At-risk learner alert, missed milestone, critical administrative action needed.

### Background & Surface Hierarchy
Surfaces rise through opacity and chromatic elevation:
- Canvas background: `#090B12` with atmospheric radial bleeds of violet glow (`rgba(99, 59, 243, 0.15)` at `50% 0%`).
- Card/module container: `#101424` with 60% opacity when paired with backdrop filters.
- Active or hovered module: `#171D33` with elevated border stroke luminosity.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy is tuned for cognitive ease and high visibility. With an adult educator audience in mind, micro-text below 12px is avoided except for compact status tags. 

- **Primary & Reading Hierarchy (DM Sans):** Selected for its geometric clarity, tall x-height, and open counters. This guarantees effortless scanning of lesson plans, learner profiles, and multi-paragraph rubrics.
- **Pedagogical Badges & Data Hierarchy (Space Grotesk):** Provides a crisp, technical cadence for module codes (e.g., `PED-402`), timestamping, grade weights, and state badges.
- **High-Contrast Readability Standards:** Primary text achieves an AAA contrast ratio on `#090B12`. Secondary copy is held strictly at `#A5ADC6` to prevent optical fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout utilizes a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid with generous margins to keep instructional dashboards and classroom views focused and breathable.

### Breakpoints & Adaptability
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, 48px (`3rem`) outer margins. Dedicated split panes for cohort navigation, gradebook matrices, and syllabus timelines.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px outer margins. Collapsible left-hand curriculum drawer; double-column pedagogical cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 20px (`1.25rem`) outer margins. Single column stacked layout. All primary action bars dock to the bottom thumb zone with a minimum target height of 48px to serve on-the-go teachers.

### Pedagogical Rhythms
- Card padding defaults to `space-md` (20px) on mobile and `space-lg` (32px) on desktop to provide generous whitespace around complex grading criteria and student performance metrics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses luminous depth, semi-translucent glass, and atmospheric lighting instead of traditional heavy drop shadows.

1. **Surface 0 (Base Abyssal Canvas):** Flat `#07080D` overlaid with subtle fixed radial gradients: `radial-gradient(ellipse 60% 40% at 50% -10%, rgba(99, 59, 243, 0.22), transparent 70%)`.
2. **Surface 1 (Card & Section Containers):** Dark frosted glass (`rgba(16, 20, 36, 0.72)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle 1px stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Surface 2 (Interactive Floating Controls & Modals):** Elevated glass (`rgba(23, 29, 51, 0.90)`), 1px border `rgba(99, 59, 243, 0.35)`, backed by a localized ambient aura: `box-shadow: 0 12px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6), 0 0 24px -2px rgba(99, 59, 243, 0.25)`.
4. **Active Focus & Attention State:** Soft radiant outer pulse (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.35)`), ensuring immediate orientation for educators presenting or navigating during lectures.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced modern radius model (`roundedness: 2`):
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius. Delivers a clean, professional instrument feel without harsh sharp edges.
- **Cards, Cohort Modules & Dialogs:** 16px (`1rem`) radius (`rounded-lg`), imparting a tactile, friendly surface.
- **Badges, Pills & Quick Status Chits:** Full pill-shape (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish categorical meta-tags and competencies from interactive cards and inputs.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Targets
- **Large Touch Bounds:** Minimum touch target height of 48px for adult accessibility.
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#633BF3` with an inner top highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `Space Grotesk` medium/bold. On hover, produces an indigo aura (`0 0 20px rgba(99, 59, 243, 0.5)`).
- **Secondary CTA:** Frosted surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, text `#F7F8FC`.
- **Pedagogical Action Button (e.g., "Grade Cohort", "Publish Unit"):** Glowing accent borders with directional arrow indicators.

### Pedagogical Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped chips (`padding: 4px 12px`, uppercase `badge-caps` typography).
- **Competency Achieved:** Emerald glow (`background: rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.12)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.35)`, text `#00E599`).
- **In Progress / Live Session:** Cyan pulse (`background: rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.12)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(0, 229, 255, 0.35)`, text `#00E5FF`).
- **Needs Attention:** Amber alert (`background: rgba(255, 179, 0, 0.12)`, `border: 1px solid rgba(255, 179, 0, 0.35)`, text `#FFB300`).

### Cards & Lesson Units
- Encased in frosted acrylic container styling (`#101424` at 70% opacity, 16px radius).
- Features a top hairline shimmer and a luminous accent dot indicating the educational subject or unit status.
- Expandable accordion drawers for lesson plan breakdown with smooth 200ms ease-out transitions.

### Inputs & Form Controls
- Height 48px to prevent miss-clicks.
- Background `rgba(8, 10, 18, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Focused state: Border transitions to `#633BF3` with an outer soft cyan or violet glow ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 59, 243, 0.25)`).
- Labels are perpetually visible above fields in `DM Sans` 14px semi-bold (`#A5ADC6`), avoiding floating placeholders that disorient adult users.

### Checkboxes & Evaluation Rubrics
- Size: 22x22px square with a 6px corner radius.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)` with dark background.
- Checked: `#633BF3` fill with a crisp white check icon and a discrete violet halo.

### Specialized Pedagogical Elements
- **Cohort Progress Bar:** Sleek 6px track with a glowing gradient fill (Cyan `#00E5FF` to Violet `#633BF3`), displaying percentage indicators in `Space Grotesk`.
- **Live Classroom Presence Ring:** Avatar framed with an animated 2px pulsing border in emerald or cyan indicating active participation.
